About Maison Berber Ait Lhsen

Your local guide to the High Atlas & Tizi N’Oucheg

A guiding experience from Marrakech, built around one mountain village and the people who live there.

Meet Your Guide Contact Mohamed

Maison Berber Ait Lhsen is a local guiding experience from Marrakech, built around Tizi N’Oucheg village and the High Atlas Mountains.

From Marrakech, the High Atlas is closer than it looks. Our day trips lead to the mountain village of Tizi N’Oucheg and into the Ourika Valley as far as the Setti Fatma waterfalls, with walks, village visits and local hosts along the way.

Amazigh culture is part of every day out. Each trip is arranged personally with Mohamed, so the details are shaped around your group and confirmed with you before you book.

Why trust us

Why trust Maison Berber for your day trips from Marrakech?

Maison Berber Ait Lhsen is built on local knowledge rather than a big agency brochure. Our day trips, mountain walks and cultural experiences in the High Atlas are planned personally with Mohamed Ait Lhsen, who was born in Tizi N’Oucheg village.

  • Local knowledge of the High Atlas

    Mohamed knows the paths, the villages and the people around Tizi N’Oucheg and the Ourika Valley, with extensive local guiding experience. That knowledge shapes every walk, every stop and every conversation along the way.

  • Personal, tailored day trips

    No two groups are alike. Tell Mohamed about your group, your interests and the pace you prefer, and he can talk through what is possible, including adapting the itinerary.

  • Authentic Berber village experiences

    Visit traditional villages, share lunch with a local host and, where available, try a bread-making or weaving workshop. You see village life as it is lived, guided by someone who belongs to it.

  • A direct and transparent way to book

    You talk to Mohamed directly on WhatsApp. Schedules, inclusions and any costs are confirmed with you before you book, and there is no online payment on this site.

About the house

Who we are

Maison Berber Ait Lhsen is a guiding experience based in Marrakech and rooted in Tizi N’Oucheg village, in the High Atlas Mountains. It is led by Mohamed Ait Lhsen, a local mountain guide born in the village.

We organize day trips from Marrakech and village activities, each one planned with you rather than taken from a fixed template. Far from standardized tourism, the aim is a personal day that fits your group.

What drives us

Our mission

Our mission is to share the High Atlas with warmth and honesty: its landscapes, its villages and the Amazigh traditions of the people who live there.

Tell Maison Berber what you hope to discover and we will help you shape a day that is unhurried, personal and true to the mountains.

About Maison Berber: FAQ

Details are confirmed with Mohamed directly, so you always get current information.

Ask on WhatsApp
Who is Maison Berber Ait Lhsen?

Maison Berber Ait Lhsen is a guiding experience based in Marrakech and built around Tizi N’Oucheg village in the High Atlas. Every trip is led and arranged by Mohamed Ait Lhsen, who was born in the village.

Which day trips do you offer?

Two day trips from Marrakech: the Atlas Trek and Authentic Berber Village Experience to Tizi N’Oucheg, and the Ourika Valley and Setti Fatma Waterfalls Adventure. We also arrange activities such as mountain walks, a cooking class and a pottery workshop.

Do I need to be experienced to go walking in the Atlas Mountains?

The Tizi N’Oucheg trip includes a mountain walk, and trail conditions near Setti Fatma can change. Tell Mohamed about your group and he can talk through what suits you, including adapting the itinerary.

Can I visit the Setti Fatma waterfalls on a day trip from Marrakech?

Yes. The Setti Fatma waterfall area is part of our Ourika Valley and Setti Fatma Waterfalls Adventure, with local guidance. Timings and arrangements are confirmed with Mohamed when you book.

Can I arrange a trip for my own group?

Send Mohamed your preferred date, group size and interests on WhatsApp and he will confirm what is possible. There is no online payment on this site.

How do I book a day trip or ask a question?

The quickest way is WhatsApp on +212 626 543 474. You can also email mohamedaitlhcen70@gmail.com or use the contact page with your date, group size and the experience you have in mind.
