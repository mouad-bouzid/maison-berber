Day trips from Marrakech

Discover unforgettable day trips from Marrakech with Maison Berber Ait Lhsen. Leave the city for the High Atlas Mountains, walk to the traditional village of Tizi N’Oucheg, or follow the Ourika Valley to the Setti Fatma waterfalls. Mohamed Ait Lhsen, a local mountain guide in Morocco born in Tizi N’Oucheg, shares mountain landscapes, Amazigh traditions and authentic Berber village experiences with a personal welcome.

Why go

Why explore the High Atlas from Marrakech

On the road from Marrakech, the busy medina gives way to the quieter landscape of the High Atlas Mountains: terraced fields, red-earth villages and wide mountain views. It is a rich setting for cultural experiences near Marrakech.

With Mohamed, a local guide who knows the paths, a day trip is about more than the scenery. You meet village life as it is lived and learn about Amazigh culture and traditions along the way.

  • Mountain scenery and villages

    Panoramic views, cultivated terraces and traditional Amazigh villages.

  • Amazigh culture and traditions

    Everyday village life and local customs, shared by a guide from Tizi N’Oucheg.

  • Walking routes and viewpoints

    Scenic mountain paths, with a possible 4×4 alternative on the Tizi N’Oucheg trip, subject to confirmation.

  • Traditional food and workshops

    Lunch with a local host, plus pottery, bread-making or weaving workshops where available.

  • A change of pace

    The contrast between Marrakech’s busy medina and the calm of the mountains.
