Things to do around Marrakech

Looking for things to do beyond the medina? Maison Berber Ait Lhsen offers activities from Marrakech that range from High Atlas mountain walks and traditional cooking to pottery workshops, camel rides and quad rides in the Palmeraie. Mountain walks in the High Atlas

Follow mountain paths past cultivated terraces and traditional villages with a guide born in Tizi N’Oucheg. Tell Mohamed about your group and he can suggest a route that suits you.

  • Panoramic views and cultivated terraces
  • Traditional villages along the route
  • Routes around Tizi N’Oucheg and the Ourika Valley, adapted to your group

FAQ

Before you book

Details are confirmed with Mohamed directly, so you always get current information.

Ask on WhatsApp
Which activities can I book?

Mountain walks, a cooking class, a pottery workshop, camel rides and quad rides in the Palmeraie. Availability varies, so ask Mohamed which activities suit your dates.

Can I combine an activity with a day trip?

Some activities, such as pottery, also appear in our Ourika Valley day trip. If you would like to add something to your day, ask Mohamed and he will tell you what is possible.

Are the activities suitable for families?

Tell Mohamed who is coming and their ages, and he can advise which activities suit your group. For the quad ride, ask about safety equipment and minimum age.

Are prices and inclusions listed?

No prices are published on this site. Prices, inclusions and pickup details are confirmed with Mohamed before you book.

How do I book an activity?

Send a WhatsApp message with the activity you like, your preferred date and group size. Mohamed will confirm the details with you. There is no online payment on this site.
